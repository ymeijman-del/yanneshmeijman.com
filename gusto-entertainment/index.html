Gusto Entertainment

editor / since 2023




I work as a freelance editor for film distributor Gusto Entertainment that specialises in Dutch arthouse films. Gusto worked on titles like Knor, Buladó, Paradise Drifters, Met Mes, De Libi, Sweet Dreams, Onder de blote hemel, en vele andere. I edit the video content: short promos, teasers, clips, and moving posters.
